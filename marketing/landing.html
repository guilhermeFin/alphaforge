The problem

Most backtests are fiction with good production values.

Look-ahead bias. Survivorship bias. Costs that quietly vanish. And the big one: you tried fifty versions and kept the one that looked best — luck wearing a lab coat. Quantopian crowdsourced alpha from 300,000 people and shut down; the industry learned that crowdsourcing alpha mostly crowdsources overfit. The popular tools make it worse, sweeping 100,000 parameter combinations to hand you the prettiest curve. The market eventually corrects the record. It charges for the lesson.
