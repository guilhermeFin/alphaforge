The strategy library

A shelf of the classic factors every quant has heard of — each one shipped with its honesty verdict. The point isn't to hand you a winner. It's to show you what survives the trial and what doesn't.

Read this before you read a single number. Every figure on this page is illustrative output from a synthetic demo dataset, not a real track record. The demo data is built so that a quality-style edge is deliberately planted and a momentum-style edge deliberately is not. So when quality passes and momentum fails below, that proves the tool can tell them apart — it says nothing about whether quality beats momentum in live markets. Run these on your own data to find out what's true for you.

Every strategy gets the same trial

There's no special lane for the factor you're hoping works. Quality, momentum, your own custom signal — all of them face the identical gauntlet before any verdict is printed.

∆

Deflated Sharpe

A raw Sharpe ratio flatters you the more configurations you tried. The deflated version pays the multiple-testing tax back — discounting the result for every variant that was searched — so a number that only looked good by luck collapses to where it belongs.

⊟

PBO via CSCV walk-forward

Combinatorially split the history, fit on some folds, test on the rest, and measure how often the in-sample best is below median out-of-sample. That probability of backtest overfitting is the honest read on whether the edge travels or was tailored to the past.

∿

Signal IC

Before any equity curve, we check the raw correlation between the signal and the returns it's supposed to predict. Weak, unstable information coefficients are the early tell that a strategy is dressing up randomness as foresight.
