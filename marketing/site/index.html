What makes it different

The rigor is the product

Most platforms hand you a pretty equity curve and stop. AlphaForge runs the defenses the literature says actually matter — operationalized straight from the canonical texts — and only calls a strategy credible when they all pass.

Deflated & Probabilistic Sharpe

Bailey & López de Prado. The headline isn't a naive Sharpe — it's one that discounts for fat tails, track length, and how many strategies you tried.

Probability of Backtest Overfitting

CSCV (combinatorially-symmetric cross-validation). Answers the question that kills quant books: "given I picked the best of N, how likely is it to disappoint out-of-sample?"

Point-in-time, purged & embargoed

López de Prado's purge + embargo on every walk-forward fold. A value exists only from the date it was knowable; the past never moves.

Fat-tail-aware risk

Blitzstein's probability lessons as code — historical CVaR, skew/kurtosis, and base-rate guards, because Gaussian risk numbers lie when tails are fat.

Factor attribution

Every strategy is regressed on Fama-French / Carhart factor portfolios — so a "great" return that's really just cheap factor beta is exposed, not sold to you as alpha.

Honest ML, not hype

An optional model ladder — regularized linear → boosted trees → stacking — under purged cross-validation, reporting whether complexity actually beats the simple baseline out-of-sample. Usually it doesn't, and the tool says so.

The verdict

Every run ends in a judgment

CREDIBLE or NOT CREDIBLE — decided out-of-sample first, with the deflated Sharpe, the overfitting probability, and the honesty haircuts spelled out on the page. No cherry-picking, no hidden assumptions.

  • Out-of-sample walk-forward first, not full-sample
  • Trial counter deflates the Sharpe automatically
  • The methodology travels with the result
Signal quality

Judge the signal, not just the curve

A good backtest can come from a bad signal plus luck. Every signal carries its information coefficient, IC decay, and out-of-sample sign stability — so a lucky curve on a meaningless signal can't sneak through.

  • Information coefficient with significance t-stat
  • IC decay across horizons
  • Rolling-window sign stability across eras
Reporting
