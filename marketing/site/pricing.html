FAQ

Questions

Is the free tier crippled?

No. The entire honesty engine — Deflated Sharpe, PBO, walk-forward with purge/embargo, the trial ledger, signal IC, CVaR — runs on Free. What paid tiers add is better data and the AI copilot, not a better backtest. We will never sell you a more flattering result.

Why are paid tiers priced around data?

Point-in-time fundamental data (SimFin, Sharadar, Capital IQ/Compustat) carries real third-party licensing cost, and it's the input that makes fundamental backtests trustworthy. We pass that through transparently rather than bundling a markup into a vague "Pro" plan.

Is this investment advice?

No. AlphaForge is research software. It never emits personalized or specific buy/sell/price-target output — it's a lab you drive, like a spreadsheet or a charting terminal. Backtested results are hypothetical and not indicative of future performance.

What's a "workspace" and the trial ledger?

A workspace is one continuous research session. The trial ledger counts how many distinct strategies you've tested in it, so the Deflated Sharpe is deflated by your real search size — you can't quietly cherry-pick the best of fifty and call it one trial.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the period end; annual plans are billed yearly. Your data and saved workspaces export cleanly.
